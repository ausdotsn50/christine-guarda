There’s not much to say when the origin of sin is placed on the first woman. No sound of defense was made when she was blamed for the transgression that occurred. The forbidden fruit was forbidden without reason, and even if there was one, no amount of explanation would have made sense to minds that could not discern between good and evil without completing the very act that was prohibited. There’s not much to say and nothing to be done, for since the beginning of time, a woman has not had the discretion to know what lies ahead of her because, as the believers have said, it is all part of His plan, and we are in the dark of His blinding light.
