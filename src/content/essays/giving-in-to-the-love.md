Fire was made. Fire was discovered. Fire was given. There are many tales of the origin of heat, but the thing that I find most vital in these stories is that fire was received and sustained. Fire was used, and fire was made useful. Fire shone and brought warmth, and fire dwindled and brought pain. Fire was many things, and it is oftentimes found within—in the hearth of a home or in the heart of a person. Fire is meant to reveal humanity’s flesh, for we are meant to burn. I would like to believe that as we first beheld fire, it touched our skin and peeled back our layers to unveil the colors of the flames that are inherently there in our bodies.
