Let there be light, and let there be darkness. Allow both things to exist accordingly, and make yourself appreciate the beauty of the overlooked. Conjure a taste so sweet that it covers the original bitterness. Nothing on earth is as valuable as itself. Let your sight perceive the beauty of tiny objects and mundane activities. Nothing is unworthy of attention—the leaves, the dust, the half-emptied cup. In the grand scheme of things, the tiniest bit is as significant as the largest, for everything has its purpose, and everything matters.
