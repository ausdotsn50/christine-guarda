Can love exist in the middle of a catastrophe? I often ask myself, uncertain whether love can truly persist if the end of the world were to come. I found the answer as I looked at my sister’s sleeping face while scrolling on my laptop and seeing the world in havoc.

The next day, I will learn of another heinous thing humankind has done, and afterwards, as if nothing happened, I will laugh with my friends—a cacophony of happy pigeons on an electrical wire.

The truth is simple—I will continue to love and be loved amidst the destruction of this realm. Love will not just exist, but it will endure. It will be present in the ugliest of places. And as for me, I will exist for it. I will keep on spreading it like a disease with no cure.

For what am I if I don’t love? What am I if I’m not loved? What is the world without love?
