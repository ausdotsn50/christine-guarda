An illness requires a cure or an antidote to counter the poison. But what I have is neither an illness nor a poison. What I have is not a tangible thing that can be removed or dissected. It is a matter of my being, sewn into my very heart, glued into my very soul. Many believe themselves to be experts on a phenomenon that is widely known to be a source of everyone’s foolishness. How foolish and depressing it is to see individuals presenting themselves as healers of a disease that does not exist, creating conflicts that should not even be present in the first place. It is my hope to find a cure for them—to help their impaired eyes see the flaws in the way they perceive individuals who share my woes. Be that as it may, awareness itself is my flaw, for I am certain that just as doctors and scientists cannot conjure a medicine in only a fortnight, it is also impossible to make nonbelievers accept the reality that there is no cure for someone like me.
