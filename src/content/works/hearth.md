A short documentary about the kitchens that hold a family together: the people who cook, the recipes they keep, and the conversations that only happen around a stove. I wrote the narration and shaped the interviews into a story.

We filmed over three weekends in Legazpi, and most of the writing happened in the edit, finding the line that tied each voice to the next.
