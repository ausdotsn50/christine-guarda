A radio documentary exploring issues in the education sector, written and produced as a final requirement for “Broadcasting and Journalism Principles and Practices”. I served as Head Writer and contributed to the development of the documentary’s script. The project received the Merit Award at the 11th Philippine Student Quill Awards under Division 4: Audio-Visual (Category 22).
