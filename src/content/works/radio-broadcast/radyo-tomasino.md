A student-led radio program that ran for two seasons during A.Y. 2025–2026, featuring discussions on the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and conversations with student and professional guests. The program provided a platform for diverse perspectives on local, national, and global issues. I served as Head Writer for both seasons, Production Staff during Season 2 recordings, and Assistant Director for a special Facebook Live episode.
