A radio magazine titled “Sa Totoo Lang”, written and produced as a midterm requirement for “Broadcasting and Radio Principles and Practices”. The program tackles current national issues, with me serving as Head Writer. It received the Excellence Award at the 11th Philippine Student Quill Awards in Division 4: Audio-Visual (Category 22) and Division 1: Media Relations (Category 9).
