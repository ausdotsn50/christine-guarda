A collaborative magazine or literary folio produced by the third-year Communication class as a final requirement for “Digital Publishing”. Each student was tasked with contributing at least three pages, which could feature articles, creative fiction, photobooks, advertisement designs, or other forms of content. Students were also responsible for designing their own pages.
