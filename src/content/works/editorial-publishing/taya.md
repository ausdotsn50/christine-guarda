“Taya” is my final quiz output, centered on the concept of showcasing Filipino traditional and contemporary games (Larong Pinoy). The task required us to develop a magazine concept, including its name, an explanation of its overall theme, and the design concept for its inside pages, as well as create an original cover art design for the proposed magazine.
