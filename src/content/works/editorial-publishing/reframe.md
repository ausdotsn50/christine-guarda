A magazine produced as a midterm requirement, featuring an original concept developed by the class. Each student contributed to the writing process and took on a role within the editorial board. I served as Managing Editor and Writer and contributed to the magazine’s overall content. The project received the Excellence Award at the 11th Philippine Student Quill Awards under Division 4: Publications (Category 24).
