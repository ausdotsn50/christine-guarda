As a final requirement for NSTP 2 under the Literary Training Service component, Communication students were tasked with creating a literary folio documenting their immersion experience at Arimbay Elementary School. I served as Editorial Assistant and Writer, contributing two articles, one of which I co-wrote with my classmate and friend, Donna Zipagan.
